Case File

A restless city. A new detective. Too many cases.

You have just been assigned to the precinct on the edge of the Old Market. Crime never stops here, and the cases pile up faster than anyone can close them. Every criminal who walks free makes the city a little darker, and remembers your name.

How to Play

Cards and verbs

Everything is a card. You act by dragging cards into the verbs along the top. Each verb has a first slot; filling it reveals the others. When the verb says what it will do, press Begin.

Cases

Cases arrive on their own and count down. When one hits zero it goes cold, the criminal is At Large, and Public Pressure rises. You can't close everything.

Detective work

Investigate a case to search the scene, or with its District to canvass. Analyze evidence. Interrogate witnesses and suspects with Focus, Instinct or Health. Reflect on a case with its clues and see what they add up to.

Charges

Clues carry aspects: Forensic, Testimony, Motive, Opportunity, Digital, Financial. Arrest a suspect with clues from their case. The right aspects in enough weight make a solid charge. A thin one may walk, and they won't forget you.

Read everything

Suspects have traits. Clues sometimes leak the culprit's. A careful reader can catch the culprit before the mind palace does.

Threats

Fatigue ×3 becomes Burnout. Obsession ×3 becomes Tunnel Vision, and you misread clues. Public Pressure, Scrutiny (Internal Affairs) and Retaliation build up at the top. Rest in Reflect. Close cases. Keep your hands clean, or don't.

Growing

Spend Funds in Requisition on equipment, precinct rooms and new officers. Reputation brings promotion boards, and new verbs: Warrant, Stakeout, Undercover, Task Force.

Winning

Your Calling decides how you win. Its card on the table tells you what it needs. Rent comes out every week. Don't go broke.
