How to Play

Cards and verbs

Everything is a card. The verbs are the square tokens on the table; move them like cards. Drag a card onto a verb, or tap the verb to open it at the side of the table. A small box appears on a token only when it has something to show: the card the verb holds, or the magnet when cards on the table fit its open slots (it glows; tapping it pulls them in) (never your Health, Wit, Instinct or Coin: those are yours to choose). The Bell's box shows the dues it is about to draw: lodging, and a Coin for every two watchmen you keep. Tap a verb to open it: its first slot takes a card, and filling it reveals the others. Cards that fit glow, and tapping an empty slot lists every card on the table that fits it. When the verb says what it will do, press the button that names it. Some combinations fit the slots but lead nowhere, at least not yet.

What a verb finds

A finished verb shows a card back on its token. Open it: what it found lies face down. Tap a card to turn it over, tap it again to take it, or Take all. Closing the window leaves everything where it is, story included. Taken cards join a stack of their kind if one is on the table, else they land in the collection pile, the yellow strip; drag the strip anywhere. New cases land there too.

Your table

Move any card or stack anywhere on the table; with Cards settle on the grid on (Settings, Table) they land on an invisible grid's cells; Show the grid draws it if you want to see it. Any two cards that read the same stack: drop one on the other; drag the top card off a stack to take one, or drag the stack by its number (or Shift-drag) to move all of it. Drag the table to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, 0 to fit. The corner tools Stack like cards together and Tidy the whole table; Undo puts them back. The verb window stands at the side; tapping the table, a card or another verb puts it away.

Time

Space pauses; 1, 2, 3 set the speed. You can still move cards and start verbs while paused. Settings can pause the game for you when a case arrives, a verb finishes, or while you drag. The ring around a card is its clock, and it stops while the card is inside a verb. Clues and witnesses warn you half a minute before they fade; tap the warning to find them. The Bell says when the next pay and lodging fall due.

Keys

S or Enter starts the open verb (on a finished one: turns its finds over, then takes them). C collects every finished verb. Esc closes it. Tab stacks, T tidies, Z undoes that, J opens the journal, + and − zoom.

Cases

Cases arrive on the city's clock and count down. When one runs out it goes Unanswered, the culprit is Abroad, and the Crowd grows louder. You cannot answer everything.

Six verbs

Attend is the Watch-house: Wit keeps the day-book for a Coin, Health walks the hard round for two, Petitions are bought there, and it deals with what lands on the desk. Explore is the city: a case to search its scene, a case with its Quarter to go door to door, Instinct alone to walk the ward. Take raw proof to Study to make it speak. Question witnesses and the accused with Wit, Instinct or Health. Rest a case with its tokens and see what they add up to, or rest.

The Court

Tokens carry proof of six kinds: Body, Word, Motive, Presence, Writ, Coin. An accused with tokens from their case in The Court is a charge. The right kinds in enough weight make full proof. Indicia alone will not convict; on half proof the accused may walk, and they will not forget you. A conviction makes a Condemned card and lays the ladder beside it; the Condemned with a rung in The Court is a sentence.

The ladder

Pardon, a Fine, the Pillory, Banishment, Branding, the Sword, the Rope, the Wheel. A plea or a free confession is a reason for mercy. Every rung has its price in Mercy, Cruelty and Dread. Say nothing and the Council sentences by custom.

The question

Question the accused with Health and everybody confesses; only the guilty tell the truth. The Carolina allows it only on sufficient indicia, and the Court takes the confession only when Body or Writ agrees. Two witnesses who agree for different reasons are full proof; a beaten witness is not credible.

Patrons and the Court

Some cases are commissions: the Council, the Bishop or the Guilds want a particular answer, and Favour follows. The Coquille has a court under the Warrens: break it, parley with its King (the Coquille with Wit in Explore), or stand its trial (with Instinct and Coin) and stay inside. The purse is always open, and every coin that changes the truth is counted.

Read everything

The accused have their marks. Tokens sometimes betray the culprit's. A careful reader can name the culprit before the casebook does.

Threats

Weariness ×3 becomes Fever. Obsession ×3 becomes Fixation, and you misread proof. The Crowd, the Council's Suspicion and the underworld's Vendetta build up at the top. Rest in Rest. Answer cases. Keep your hands clean, or don't.

Rising

The Market opens after your first conviction and the Petitions after your second. Spend Coin in Attend on instruments, Watch-house rooms and watchmen. Standing brings the Council's letters, and each office opens something inside the verbs: Sworn Examiner, a Writ (an accused with cause in Explore); Bailiff, the Watch and Disguise (Explore) and Deputise (Attend); Magistrate, Muster and Proclamation (Attend). The ⌂ button shows your Watch-house: rooms that change how the verbs work.

Winning

Your Calling decides how you win. Its card on the table tells you what it needs. Lodging and dues come out at every bell. Do not run dry.
