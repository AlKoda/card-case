# Ten games beside this one

What each does that this game could use, and whether it was taken. The
rule: take quality of life and clarity; leave anything that would turn the
desk into a chore, spoil the reading of a case, or trade evidence for coin.

| # | Game | What it has | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cultist Simulator | Verbs, cards with aspects, timers: the ancestor. Tapping an aspect in a card's detail lights up every card that carries it. Its doom spirals (Dread, Fascination). | **Taken:** the aspect popover's *Show on the table* lights every table card with that kind of proof. **Left:** the doom spirals; Fever and the needs already press, and more would be a chore. |
| 2 | Book of Hours | A compendium of everything learned; a verb's known recipes listed. | **Taken:** a verb's *i* pane lists the ways you have found there, with counts. **Left:** a full codex screen; the journal and the dossiers already hold it. |
| 3 | Stacklands | Card stacking on a free table; a list of small goals (firsts) to tick; selling cards for coin; an idea card shows a recipe. | **Taken:** *Firsts*, a short list of milestones at the top of the journal. **Left:** selling cards (evidence for coin would break the economy and the reading of a case); recipe cards (the verbs already preview what a card will do). |
| 4 | Card Survival: Tropical Island | Many fine-grained needs; skills that grow by doing; an end-of-day summary. | **Taken:** the Bell's story now ends with the week's ledger: closed cases, open cases, coin in hand. **Left:** more needs; three with clocks is enough for a desk that is not a survival sim. |
| 5 | The Case of the Golden Idol / Obra Dinn | Fill-in deduction: a fixed set of blanks the game checks at once. | **Left:** it would replace the Court's charge (proof of kinds, in weight) with a quiz; the accused's marks and the tokens' traits already let a careful reader name the culprit first. |
| 6 | Reigns | Each answer shows which meters it will move before you choose. | **Taken already** (round 6): every answer says what it gives and what it takes. **Left:** arrows on the meters themselves; the words are the design. |
| 7 | Sunless Sea / Fallen London | Storylets gated by qualities; odds shown on a choice ("a modest chance"). | **Left:** the choices here are answered, not rolled; the Court already shows the charge's strength in words (thin, half, full). |
| 8 | Papers, Please | A day loop with a ledger at its end; a rulebook in play. | **Taken:** the ledger (see 4); Help is the rulebook and grew a Growing section. **Left:** fail states on the clock; cases going cold is the pressure. |
| 9 | Disco Elysium | Thoughts internalised over time; skills that talk. | **Taken already:** Insights, lessons and tricks, and how each grows is now listed on the ability. **Left:** talking abilities; the cards keep few words. |
| 10 | Shadows of Doubt | An evidence board: pinned notes and strings between them. | **Taken:** *Mark* a card from its dossier, and a pin shows on its corner (the case strings already run between a case and its cards). **Left:** free-text notes; typing on a phone at the table is not the game. |
