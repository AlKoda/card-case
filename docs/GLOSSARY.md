# Arabic glossary for Case File: The Free City (المدينة الحرة)

Register: formal Modern Standard Arabic (الفصحى), literary, with the tone of a
16th‑century chronicle. Address the player in the second person masculine
singular (أنتَ) as the conventional neutral; prefer constructions that avoid
gender where natural. Keep Western digits (1, 2, 3). Use Arabic punctuation
(،  ؛  ؟) and keep full stops. Keep every {placeholder} and every HTML tag
(<b>, <i>) exactly as in the key. Never translate the key side.

## Counts in words
An ending reads its counts aloud ('Seven times you sent a poor sinner home ... and four of them').
The number words one to twelve are entries of their own, in the masculine form that counts people
(سبعة, أربعة), and the sentence is built so that form fits: عددُهم سبعة، وأربعة منهم.
